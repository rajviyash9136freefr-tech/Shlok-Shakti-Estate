## Key Features Added

1. **Embedded Google Map & Direct Directions:**
   - Interactive responsive Google Map iframe embedded directly in Section 09 (Contact Section).
   - "OPEN IN GOOGLE MAPS" direct button routing to `https://maps.app.goo.gl/DPScRmfAKrGRVddP6`.
2. **Floating Quick-Contact Action Bar:**
   - Permanent one-tap WhatsApp and Phone call buttons floating at the bottom right for instant lead conversion.
3. **Interactive Property Detail Modals:**
   - Real-time pop-up modal showcasing full specifications, super area, configurations, and direct enquiry autofill.
4. **Front-End Validated Enquiry Form:**
   - Clean validation for Full Name, Email, Phone, Preferred Location, and Investment Budget in Indian numbering format (Lakhs & Crores).
5. **Strict Two-Color Editorial Luxury Design:**
   - Obsidian Black (`#171717`) and Ivory (`#F3EFE6`) with typography pairing (`Cormorant Garamond` and `Inter`).
